About

GradientLab fills a layer with a gradient you design. Apply it to any shape, text, solid or footage layer and the gradient fills the layer's shape. Every part of it can be keyframed, the gradient itself included. On a text layer it looks finished right away: deep colors bent into the letters, a soft glow and fine grain.

Features

  • Looks finished on textThe first time it goes on a text layer it applies the Luminous style; the Style menu applies Luminous or Flat to any layer
  • Seven gradient typesLinear, Radial, Angular, Magnet, Contour, Mask Path, and Luminance (a gradient map of the layer)
  • A gradient editor in Effect ControlsClick to add a stop, drag to move it, double-click for its color; keyframe the whole gradient and the stops glide between keyframes
  • Palettes and a library15 palettes, Randomize Colors, Reverse, and a Library of your own gradients, shared with Transporter
  • Better color blendsRGB, Linear Light, OKLab or OKLCH, with a choice of hue path, linear or smooth
  • Colors from a layerUse a row of any photo, video or precomp as the gradient, or a random row per stripe
  • StripesSolid or gradient stripes with random offsets, breaks, gaps, soft edges and noise distortion
  • Depth and turbulenceBend the color bands into domes, then ripple them with looping noise
  • Glow, grain and soft focusA linear-light glow, film grain, and optical diffusion around a sharp focus area
  • Built to animateCycles, Offset, a seamless Loop Phase and a Speed that moves it with no keyframes; 8, 16 and 32-bit color, multithreaded

Setup

  1. Install. Run the installer. It asks for administrator permission, since After Effects plug-ins live in a shared folder.
  2. Restart After Effects. Start it, or restart it if it was already open.
  3. Apply the effect. Select a text or shape layer and choose Effect → GradientLab → GradientLab. Text layers get the Luminous style right away.
  4. Make it yours. Pick a Palette, or click the gradient bar to add stops and double-click one to change its color. Choose a Type under Gradient Shape.
  5. Animate it. Keyframe Offset or Loop Phase, set a Speed, or turn on the stopwatch next to the gradient itself.

If the installer won't open

The installers aren't signed yet.

macOS: right-click the installer and choose Open. On macOS 15 and later, open System Settings → Privacy & Security instead and click Open Anyway.

Windows: if SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

Advanced and uninstalling

Effects on the color layer

Colors From: Layer reads the Color Layer's source, before its own effects. To sample a layer with its effects, precompose it first.

Where it installs

macOS: to uninstall, delete this folder.

/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Common/Plug-ins/7.0/MediaCore/GradientLab

Windows: to uninstall, use Settings → Apps → GradientLab for After Effects, or run Uninstall GradientLab.exe in this folder.

C:\Program Files\Adobe\Common\Plug-ins\7.0\MediaCore\GradientLab

Requirements

After Effects on macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel), or on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Developed and tested on After Effects 2026; earlier versions haven't been tested. Everything runs locally: no accounts, and no network calls except sending a gradient to Transporter on your own machine when you ask it to.
